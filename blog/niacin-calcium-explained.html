Niacin & Calcium In MemoHoney Explained

Niacin and Calcium are two of the more familiar nutrients in MemoHoney's formula, each playing a distinct supporting role.

Niacin: Supporting Cellular Energy

Niacin is an essential B vitamin that supports normal energy metabolism and nervous-system function. It helps cells convert nutrients into usable energy, which is especially relevant for mental alertness and cognitive energy.

Calcium: Supporting Nerve Signaling

Calcium is widely known for bone health, but it also plays an essential role in normal nerve signaling and cellular communication — supporting healthy nervous-system function relevant to concentration and overall cognitive wellness.

Two Different Roles, One Formula

Niacin's energy-metabolism role and Calcium's nerve-signaling role complement the circulation support from L-Arginine and L-Citrulline — see our circulation ingredients breakdown.

A Note On Evidence

The available research concerns Niacin and Calcium individually, not the finished MemoHoney product. Always check the Supplement Facts panel on your physical bottle for exact amounts.
