Who Should Talk To A Doctor Before Trying MemoHoney

MemoHoney's label includes specific guidance on who should check with a doctor first. Here's a closer look at who that includes.

If You're Pregnant Or Nursing

MemoHoney's label advises that pregnant or nursing mothers consult a physician before using this or any dietary supplement.

If You're Under 18

MemoHoney is intended for adults; its label specifies that children under the age of 18 should consult a physician before use.

If You Have A Known Medical Condition

Anyone with a known medical condition should review the full ingredient list — see our Ingredients page — with their healthcare provider before starting.

Do Not Exceed The Recommended Dose

MemoHoney's label specifies one capsule daily; exceeding the recommended dose is not advised. If you take other medications, it's always worth confirming with your doctor that a new supplement fits safely alongside them.
