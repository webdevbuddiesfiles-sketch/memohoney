L-Arginine & L-Citrulline: How Circulation Supports Focus

Two of MemoHoney's seven named ingredients — L-Arginine and L-Citrulline — work on healthy circulation. Here's what's behind them.

L-Arginine: Supporting Nitric Oxide Production

L-Arginine is an amino acid the body uses to help produce nitric oxide, which supports normal blood flow and circulation — helping deliver oxygen and nutrients throughout the body.

L-Citrulline HCl & L-Citrulline Malate: Working Alongside Arginine

L-Citrulline HCl supports the body's natural production of arginine and nitric oxide, while L-Citrulline Malate combines citrulline with malate to complement circulation, cellular activity and energy support.

Why Circulation Matters For Focus

Healthy blood flow helps deliver oxygen and nutrients to the brain, which is part of why MemoHoney pairs circulation-focused ingredients with its other nutrients — see our circulation and brain health piece for more on that connection.

Where They Fit In The Full Formula

L-Arginine and L-Citrulline sit alongside Niacin, Calcium and Beta Alanine in MemoHoney's complete blend — see the full list on our Ingredients page.
